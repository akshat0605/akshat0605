**Electronics & Embedded Hardware Intern** @ NavX Energy · **Overall Coordinator** @ Robotics Club IITG

I work at the intersection of power electronics, embedded firmware, and robotics — designing systems that are physically real, not just simulated. At NavX, I built a 72V/13kW PDU handling 250A peaks, a galvanically isolated CAN-bus telemetry board, and a pre-charge safety stack from scratch. For Yuvaan, IIT Guwahati's Mars rover, I designed the multi-rail power system and custom PCBs that ran through IRC '26 Finals at MIT Manipal.

My edge: I can take a project from schematic → PCB → firmware → deployment, and I understand the energy constraints behind every design decision.
